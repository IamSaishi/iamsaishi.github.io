Hi, I’m Saishi — a security researcher, penetration tester, and bug bounty hunter with hands-on experience in offensive and defensive security. I’ve spent years exploring the world of cybersecurity, from finding real-world vulnerabilities to analyzing how systems can be defended effectively.

This blog is my space to share what I learn, experiment with, and document. Here, you’ll find:

- Bug bounty write-ups: Detailed reports and insights from vulnerabilities I’ve discovered in web and mobile applications.

- CTF challenges: Step-by-step breakdowns of Capture The Flag exercises, including Android, web, and network exploitation challenges.

- Offensive security research: Practical explorations of exploitation techniques, attack methodologies, and advanced penetration testing workflows.

- Defensive insights: Lessons learned from protecting systems, analyzing attacks, and improving security posture in enterprise environments.

I aim to make security research practical, clear, and useful — whether you’re another researcher, a developer, or someone curious about how systems fail and how to fix them. My focus is always on hands-on learning, reproducible techniques, and bridging the gap between attacking and defending systems.

Outside of writing, I work professionally as a Security Engineer, responsible for penetration testing, vulnerability assessments, Android and web application testing, security reporting, and operational security. I’m also actively involved in bug bounty programs, constantly sharpening my skills and discovering new approaches to real-world security challenges.

If you’re a recruiter, a potential collaborator, or just curious: this blog is the best way to see my methodology, my work, and my approach to security research. Everything I share here reflects real experience and a practical, hands-on mindset that I bring to every project.
